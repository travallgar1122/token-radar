Methodology — how every number on this page is made

Token Radar is one program (main.ts, Node, standard library only) reading one sealed snapshot of the protocol universe and writing this board. Nothing is hand-picked and nothing is fetched at run time: given a future snapshot, the same program produces the radar for protocols that do not exist yet. Every figure below comes from the snapshot's own fields — slug, name, category, chains, TVL, 7-day TVL change, market cap, listing date — plus the snapshot's watchlist and TVL floor.

Who gets ranked

A protocol is pre-token when the snapshot records no token for it: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. Pre-token protocols are where an airdrop can still exist; a protocol that already trades has mostly spent that upside. The board carries every pre-token protocol outside the CEX category with TVL above zero, and — by the coverage rule — every watchlist protocol the snapshot contains.

The implied airdrop pool

The headline number is the model's estimate of the pool a launch would distribute:

pool = TVL × FDV multiple(category) × 8%
implied pool = pool × pre-token factor × momentum × maturity × category prior × watchlist boost, clamped to [$1, $1T]

  • FDV multiple — what a launch might price the protocol at, as a multiple of its TVL: 1.2 for venues and launchpads (DEXs, derivatives, options, launchpads, AI), 0.9 for the middle of the table, 0.5 for chains, exchanges and payment rails whose TVL is mostly pass-through.
  • Airdrop share — 8%, an assumption. Community distributions commonly land in the 5–10% band of launch valuation; the model takes 8% and says so, rather than hiding the guess inside the arithmetic.
  • Pre-token factor — 1.0 when no token is recorded; 0.7 when a symbol or CoinGecko id exists but no market cap yet; once a market cap exists it fades with the mcap/TVL ratio: 0.55 at ≤0.5, 0.40 at ≤1, 0.22 at ≤3, 0.10 above. The more the market already prices the token, the less a farming position can still capture.
  • Momentum — 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%) × 0.5. TVL rushing in is the crowd arriving early; a shrinking protocol is discounted, never excluded.
  • Maturity — 0.55 + min(age, 900 days)/900 × 0.45, where age runs from the snapshot's listing date; an unknown age counts as 300 days. A protocol that has survived longer is more likely to reach a launch at all.
  • Category prior — a multiplier between 0.5 and 1.15 reflecting how airdrop-friendly each category's launches have historically been: launchpads 1.15, farms 1.10, restaking 1.08, DEXs / lending / yield 1.05, bridges / CDP / derivatives / liquid staking 1.00, indexes / privacy / RWA 0.90, payments 0.85, chains 0.70, exchanges 0.50, anything unlisted 0.90.
  • Watchlist boost — 1.2× for protocols on the snapshot's own watchlist: the curators' flag is evidence, weighted as a mild prior.

Worked example — the board's top row, in full

Binance staked ETH (Liquid Staking, on Ethereum and Binance; listed 2023-04-29, so 1,295 days old at the snapshot) carries the largest farmable TVL in the snapshot. Here is its number, factor by factor:

  • TVL $7,448,338,841 × FDV multiple 0.9 (the middle band) → launch valuation ≈ $6,703,504,957
  • × airdrop share 8% → pool ≈ $536,280,397
  • × pre-token factor 1.00 — the snapshot records no token for it: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol
  • × momentum 0.9284 — its 7-day TVL change is −14.31%, and 1 + (−14.31% × 0.5) = 0.9284
  • × maturity 1.00 — at 1,295 days it is past the 900-day cap
  • × category prior 1.00 (Liquid Staking), and no watchlist boost — it is not on the snapshot's watchlist

implied pool = $536,280,397 × 1.00 × 0.9284 × 1.00 × 1.00 ≈ $497,900,686

Factors are shown rounded to four decimals; the program multiplies at full precision, so $497,900,686 is the exact figure the board carries for this row.

The display score (0–100)

A blend of the same signals, for scanning: 30% pre-token factor, 25% log-scaled TVL size (0 at $100K, 1 near $2B), 20% momentum, 15% category prior, 10% chain footprint (breadth, plus a small bonus for an Ethereum mainnet presence), ×1.1 on the watchlist. Rows are ranked by implied pool; the score breaks no tie the value does not.

The farm plans

Each row's six steps are generated, not written: one core action chosen by category (supply collateral for lending, restake for restaking, add liquidity for DEXs, bridge and stay for bridges, margin-trade small and weekly for derivatives, and so on), sized by TVL tier — $250 of starter capital over $1B of TVL, $150 over $100M, $75 over $10M, $40 below — held for a period set by the protocol's age (45 days under six months old, 35 under a year, 28 beyond; 35 when the age is unknown) while checking the protocol's own points or rewards surface, and repeated weekly. The weekly and claim steps each use one of four phrasings, picked deterministically from the protocol's slug, so the plans do not read as one text repeated down the board; the substance never changes — farm from one clean wallet with its seed phrase offline, and claim only from the link the protocol's own site publishes, never a search ad or a DM.

Limitations, stated plainly

  • An implied pool is a model estimate of a future event that may never happen. Many protocols on this board will never launch a token; some will launch and distribute nothing to farmers.
  • The pool is not a personal payout. One wallet's share depends on how many wallets farm, on time- and balance-weighting, and on eligibility rules the protocol has not published yet — and may never publish.
  • TVL is an imperfect signal: it can be mercenary, double-counted across restaking chains, or gone next week. The snapshot is one moment; this board is exactly as stale as its date line says.
  • The category priors and the 8% share are judgement calls, documented above so they can be disagreed with. Different assumptions produce a different board; the code makes them one-line changes.

Nothing on this page is financial advice, and no number here is a promise of a token, an airdrop, or a return. Farm only with money you can afford to lock up and lose, and treat every unsolicited claim link as a scam until the protocol's own site proves otherwise.
